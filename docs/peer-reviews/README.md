# Reviews we received on the draft data product

Three peer reviews and one mentor review of our Module 6 submission. In the course these arrive through the peer
review tool. We copied them here so the feedback-closure note (`docs/feedback-closure.md`) can point at each item.

In this sample the reviews are written examples, like the team itself. They show the kind of comments a team gets.

| File | From |
|---|---|

| `review-2-reviewer-team-y.md` | Reviewer Team Y |
| `review-3-reviewer-team-z.md` | Reviewer Team Z |
| `mentor-review.md` | Our mentor |

Reviewers rated five things on a scale of 1 to 7: reproducibility, schema and ETL quality, validation
confidence, analysis-to-question fit, and documentation and recovery evidence.
